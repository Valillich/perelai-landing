# Perelai Privacy Notice

## At a glance

Perelai handles personal data in two different ways:

1. We act as a **controller** when we decide why and how to process data for our website, accounts,
   sign-in, security, support, subscriptions, our own communications and legal obligations.
2. We act as a **processor** when a business uses Perelai to manage its own clients, bookings,
   requests, operational records and communications. In that case, the business decides why it uses
   the data and should be your first contact for a privacy request about that data.

We do not sell personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and our website does not use
analytics or advertising trackers. Our main hosting is in Germany; some providers and our operator's
remote access are in other countries, as explained in section 11.

The detailed notice below controls if this summary is incomplete.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Notice explains how Perelai handles personal data relating to:

- visitors to perelai.com and other Perelai web pages;
- people who contact us or ask about our plans;
- business owners, account holders, administrative members, invited users and service performers,
  including performers whose business maintains a profile without giving them a login;
- people using public booking, request, order, reservation, status, receipt, preference or client-hub
  pages made available through Perelai;
- clients of businesses (**End Clients**) whose information a business enters or imports into
  Perelai; and
- people who receive account, service or booking communications through Perelai.

Different sections apply depending on your relationship with us. This notice does not replace a
business's own privacy notice for its clients or staff.

## 3. Perelai and business customer roles

### When Perelai is controller

We determine purposes and means for our website, account creation, sign-in, access administration,
security and abuse prevention, support and enquiries, trial and subscription administration,
Workspace Data Export security records, our own service communications, accounting and legal claims
or obligations.

### When the business is controller and Perelai is processor

A business customer determines why and how it handles End Client contacts, service history, bookings,
requests, orders, reservations, notes, imported records, performer profiles, schedules and
assignments, payment-status records and messages sent on its behalf. We process that **Customer
Personal Data** on the business's instructions under our [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa).

For a request about Customer Personal Data, contact the business you booked with or that collected
your data first. We will assist it as required. If you cannot identify or reach the business, contact
{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} and tell us the business name and the relevant interaction; we will forward or
handle the request according to our role and applicable law.

### Independent controllers

Some organisations process data for their own purposes under their own notices: Google when you
choose Google sign-in or connect Google Calendar; Paddle, the Merchant of Record for subscription
purchases; your browser vendor's push service if you enable notifications; and professional
advisers or authorities where section 10 applies.

## 4. Personal data we handle

The actual data depends on the features used.

### Website visitors

- IP address, approximate location derived from it, browser, device and request data processed by
  our hosting and network providers to deliver and protect pages;
- your language, theme and display preferences stored in your browser (see the
  [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies)).

Our website does not use analytics, advertising pixels or marketing cookies. Campaign parameters in a
link you open are not stored in your browser or passed to the app.

### Account owners, staff and invited users

- name, email address, password hash and account identifiers;
- Google sign-in identifiers and basic profile details if you choose Google sign-in;
- phone number, if you provide it;
- workspace name, address, country, province or state, currency, timezone and settings;
- role, permissions, invitations, workspace memberships and any linked performer profile;
- language and preference settings;
- sign-in, verification, security and audit events;
- evidence of accepting our Terms, Data Processing Addendum and other documents, including the
  version, time, language and method.

We store passwords only as salted hashes, never in plain text.

### Subscriptions and billing

Subscriptions are purchased through Paddle. Paddle collects the buyer's name, email, billing address,
tax or business identifiers and payment details under its own
[Privacy Notice](https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy) and
[Buyer Terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/buyer-terms). We do not receive or store full card
details. We receive and keep the information needed to administer access and records: the payer's
account, Paddle customer, subscription and transaction identifiers, plan, amounts, currency, tax,
billing period, status, refund and cancellation information, and limited payment-method details
such as the card brand where Paddle provides them. We also keep trial eligibility and trial dates.

### Support and enquiries

When you email us, including about STUDIO+, we process your contact details, the content of your
messages and attachments, and our replies. Please do not send client records, health information,
passwords or payment card details by email. An enquiry does not sign you up for marketing.

### Customer Personal Data controlled by a business

- End Client name, phone, email, address and communication preferences;
- services, appointments, visits and service history;
- requests, orders, reservations and public booking submissions, including the versions of business
  and Perelai terms presented and any acceptance recorded;
- notes and files the business adds, performer profiles, schedules and staff assignments;
- prices, amounts, payment methods and statuses, allocations, prepaid Packages and instalments;
- where Cash Drawer is enabled, cash sessions, counts, movements, differences, notes and the staff
  involved;
- records imported from CSV, vCard or Google Calendar.

The business chooses many fields and may add free text. Our Terms prohibit using Perelai for medical
records and other sensitive data, but a user may still enter such information. Please do not include
health, diagnosis, payment card, password, government identification or similarly sensitive
information in notes or booking messages.

### Google sign-in and Google Calendar

If you sign in with Google, we use the Google account identifier, email address and name that Google
provides to create and secure your account.

If a workspace owner or manager connects Google Calendar, Perelai requests read-only access to calendar
events (scope `calendar.events.readonly`) and may process the connected Google email address, calendar
and event identifiers, event titles, descriptions, times, recurrence, time zones, attendees and
locations included in events, synchronisation state and OAuth tokens. We use this data only to show,
import and synchronise events into that workspace as configured by the user. Perelai never writes to
Google Calendar.

Perelai's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it,
do not use it to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models, and do not allow humans to read
it except with your permission for support, for security or abuse investigations, or where required by
law.

Disconnecting Google Calendar deletes the stored tokens and synchronisation state from our active
systems. Appointments already imported remain business records until the business deletes them. You
can also remove Perelai's access at any time in your Google Account settings.

### Imports

For CSV and vCard imports we process the uploaded file, parsed records, preview, mapping choices,
errors and results on the business's instructions. The uploaded file and preview are deleted
automatically once the import is finished, or after 24 hours without activity.

### Workspace Data Export

An authorised Company owner may generate a Workspace Data Export: an archive of that Company's
operational data with a manifest describing its contents. It excludes passwords, tokens and secrets,
internal system data and other Companies' data. Creating and downloading require separate
confirmation. We keep limited security records about exports: who requested them, for which Company,
status and timestamps. A Company archive is not a complete response to an individual's privacy
request; see section 13.

### Coworker availability sharing

When a business enables a coworker link for shared space, linked businesses see occupied time
intervals and the business name and colour needed to coordinate availability. They do not see client
names, service details, staff identities, amounts or notes. For a solo professional, business
identity and occupied time can still be personal data.

### Device, app and notification data

The app stores sign-in and preference information in your browser, and processes app version, browser
capabilities, locale and time zone. If you enable Web Push notifications, we store the push
subscription endpoint and keys provided by your browser, the browser type and delivery status.

## 12. Retention and deletion

We keep personal data only as long as needed for its purpose. After that we delete it or make it
anonymous. Deleted data remains in backups until they are overwritten on the rotation described
below.

| Category | Retention |
|---|---|
| account and profile | while the account exists; deleted within 30 days after a confirmed account deletion request, except the records below that have their own purpose |
| sign-in and security tokens | app sign-in tokens are valid for 1 day; Google sign-in state for 10 minutes; verification, reset and invitation tokens expire automatically and are removed by scheduled cleanup |
| server, security and access logs | normally no longer than 90 days, unless needed to investigate a specific incident |
| support emails and enquiries | up to 24 months after the last message, longer only while an issue or claim is open |
| evidence of acceptance of our Terms and other documents | 3 years (1095 days) after the account or workspace concerned is deleted |
| trial, subscription and access records | while the subscription relationship exists and 3 years (1095 days) afterwards |
| Paddle transaction, invoice and payout records | for the periods required by Ukrainian tax and accounting law, generally 1095 days from submission of the related tax report, and longer while an audit or dispute is pending |
| workspace records and Customer Personal Data | as instructed by the business; in the standard booking profile, requests that do not lead to a completed service are kept for no more than 90 days after closure, and client history for no more than 24 months after the last completed visit |
| workspace after a confirmed deletion instruction | deleted from active systems within 30 days; residual backup copies deleted within the following 30 days |
| inactive workspace without a subscription | closed after 6 consecutive months in restricted mode, following at least 30 days' email notice to the owner, then deleted on the schedule above |
| minimal record of a workspace deletion | 3 years (1095 days) after completion, without the deleted workspace content |
| import files and previews | deleted when the import finishes or after 24 hours without activity; import issue reports expire after 7 days |
| Workspace Data Export | each archive is available for 24 hours; action approvals expire after 10 minutes; export security records are kept for 12 months |
| Google Calendar tokens and synchronisation state | until Calendar is disconnected or the workspace is deleted |
| notification delivery logs | 180 days; read in-app notifications 90 days; archived in-app notifications 30 days; revoked push subscriptions 30 days |
| public booking, status and confirmation links | expire automatically and are removed by scheduled cleanup after expiry |
| database backups | made daily; kept for up to 7 days in off-site storage and up to 10 days on the hosting server |

Specific records may be kept longer where a law requires it or while they are needed for an ongoing
dispute or investigation, with access restricted to that purpose.

## 14. Security

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the data, including
encrypted connections (HTTPS), salted password hashing, role-based permissions within each workspace,
separation of each workspace's data, short-lived single-use approvals for sensitive exports, daily
database backups stored off-site in the European Union, restricted administrative access by the
operator over protected connections, and an incident response process. Our
[Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa) describes these measures in more detail.

No internet service is completely secure. Please report suspected security issues to
{{SECURITY_EMAIL}} without including unnecessary personal data.

## 15. Personal data breaches

We maintain an incident process. If a breach affects Customer Personal Data, we notify the affected
business without undue delay by email to the workspace owner, as described in the DPA. Where we are
controller, we notify authorities and affected individuals when the applicable law requires it.

## 16. Sensitive data

Perelai is not designed for medical or clinical records or payment card storage. Businesses and users
must not intentionally upload diagnoses, treatment records, genetic or biometric data,
health-insurance details, full card numbers, passwords, government identity documents or other
sensitive data.

Allergy, contraindication, skin or scalp notes and some client photographs can reveal health
information even in a beauty workflow. Businesses should record only what they need and use an
appropriate system for clinical records. Because free-text fields can receive unexpected content, we
cannot state that we never receive sensitive data; we may restrict or delete unsupported content in
line with our Terms.

## 17. Children

Business accounts are only for people aged 18 or older. Public booking pages are not directed to
children. A parent or guardian should arrange a booking for a child and contact the business directly
rather than submit the child's information through the form. If you believe a child has provided
data to us, contact {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.

## 18. Automated processing

Perelai does not use artificial intelligence or profiling to make decisions about you.

Some outcomes are determined automatically by fixed rules: public booking pages check availability,
booking lead time, required fields, email confirmation and anti-abuse limits, and, depending on the
business's settings, may accept a valid request automatically; our trial rules allow one trial per
payer; and a workspace is restricted automatically when a trial or paid period ends without a
subscription. These rules implement the business's settings or our contract terms. If an automated
outcome affects you, you can ask for a person to review it: contact the business for booking outcomes
or {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} for account and subscription outcomes.
