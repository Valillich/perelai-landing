## 7. Cancel renewal

You can cancel through the subscription-management link in your Paddle confirmation email, the
verified management route in Perelai Billing settings, or [Paddle buyer support](https://paddle.net).
If those routes fail, contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} so we can help route and record your request.

For ordinary cancellation of renewal, access continues to the end of the current paid billing
period and that subscription does not renew. `[TBD: verify end-of-period configuration and

uninstalling the PWA or stopping use does not cancel a recurring subscription. Workspace closure must

### Recommended decisions, not approvals

- **R-01:** 14 days for the first paid purchase per payer, monthly and annual; usage does not void
  this voluntary offer; later charges considered individually with mandatory/provider remedies
  preserved. A 21-day no-card evaluation reduces the need for a long unconditional refund period.
  The 14-day value is a commercial proposal, not a claim that every B2B buyer has a statutory right.
  Paddle's handbook suggests 30 days; choose and approve the actual Perelai offer explicitly.
- **R-02:** ordinary cancellation ends renewal at paid-through date. For an approved full refund,
  coordinate cancellation of the affected provider subscription and removal of that paid access
  source; reconcile future billing separately from the refund adjustment. Do not restore a consumed
  trial or erase sibling subscriptions. Partial/tax-only refunds preserve subscription unless a
  separate decision says otherwise. Reconcile with Paddle's access rule before approval.
- **R-03:** cancellation and refund intake works from email/Paddle support even when login, payer
  mapping or Company closure is broken. Record received-at and route promptly; verification does not
  reset statutory deadlines. Support targets and exact closure automation remain unapproved.
