  Paddle-calculated proration and explicit preview consent. Same subscription and billing anchor.

  non-owner memberships and no live team invitations, including admin or existing-profile invites.
  The owner resolves access, bookings and open cash custody; the payer gets safe blocker counts.
  Scheduling a downgrade prevents new team access/invitations or growth beyond SOLO immediately;
  the current paid STUDIO period remains until renewal. Cancellation needs no SOLO preparation.
