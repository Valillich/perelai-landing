# United States · English · standard appointment/request notice

**Updated:** 2026-09-25. **Status:** owner-authorised final text for code integration; deployment separate.
**Country/language:** US/en. **Version:** `business-booking-US-en-v1`. **Effective:** 2026-09-25.
**Authority:** `owner-us-en-release-20260925`, the owner's explicit instruction in the current task.
**Scope:** ordinary non-medical APPOINTMENT/REQUEST, without online payments, prepayments,
cancellation/no-show fees, sale of booking information or targeted advertising.

Historical `.draft.md` filename retained for links. Read the [release record](US.en.standard.v1.release-record.md)
for applicability and evidence limits. The US country code is not a finding that every state's
rules are inapplicable. This reusable baseline is not an enterprise CCPA supplement or a
consumer-health/children's-data notice. Existing owner preview/fact confirmation remains required;
use an external Business notice when the actual processing or additional required disclosures
are not covered. No new per-state UI or separate lawyer certificate is introduced.

The provider, location, 90-day/24-month and Company 30/30 facts reuse the owner-approved UA baseline.
No blanket declaration that European law can never apply is made. The client copy uses US-focused
wording without GDPR legal bases, EU representatives or Data Act boilerplate.

## `fullText` — Business booking notice

```text
Booking Privacy Notice

1. Who we are

{{BUSINESS_NAME}} is the business responsible for the information used to handle your booking and related client record. Contact us about a booking at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}, or about personal information at {{PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

This notice covers booking through Perelai and our related client administration. This is an ordinary, non-medical booking service, not a health-information intake form. Separate activities, such as medical records or cameras at our premises, require their own applicable information. This online booking service is not directed to children under 13; an adult should arrange any booking for a child without submitting the child's personal information here. Contact us if you believe a child has submitted information so we can address it appropriately. The effective date and version of this notice appear above.

2. Information we collect and its sources

You provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, and any message you choose to send. Your request also includes the selected service, appointment details, service provider, and communication language. We create records of the request's status and history, related communications, and the terms presented and any acceptance recorded. A request may be linked to an existing client record we hold for you; an individual booking link may use information already in that record.

The form identifies the information needed to submit your request. The ordinary public booking process requires an email address to handle and confirm a booking. Without the information needed for your selected booking process, we cannot complete the online request. Additional details are optional. Please do not include health information, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information in a message.

When you use the booking page, the service also processes your IP address, request time and outcome, and technical booking and confirmation-link identifiers. These support delivery of the page, access checks, email confirmation, troubleshooting and protection against excessive or invalid requests. Your browser may store language preferences and temporary navigation or booking-session information. The Perelai Privacy Notice explains the platform operator's separate handling of technical and security information.

3. How we use information

We use booking information to respond to your request, arrange or change an appointment, communicate about it, and provide the requested service. We maintain the related client record and a limited service history to help handle repeat visits, resolve questions or disputes, and keep necessary records of the booking and terms presented. We also use information needed to respond to privacy requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Submitting a booking does not by itself sign you up for promotional messages or authorize unrelated uses of your information. This privacy notice explains our practices; it is not a request for blanket consent.

4. How information is disclosed

People authorized to arrange or provide your service can access the information needed for their work.

Perelai is operated by ILLICHOV VALERII (registered in Ukraine as ІЛЛІЧОВ ВАЛЕРІЙ ВАЛЕРІЙОВИЧ), an individual entrepreneur (FOP) registered in Ukraine, trading as “Perelai”. For booking and related client administration, the operator processes personal information on our instructions.

Ukrainian tax identification number (RNOKPP): 3278516853.
Ukrainian Unified State Register (EDR) entry number: 2001010010001028235.
Registered business address: Zamarstynivska 170E, apartment 68, Lviv, 79068, Ukraine.
Perelai support, privacy and legal enquiries: support@perelai.app.

Perelai uses Hetzner Online GmbH to host the application, database, job queues and files in Falkenstein, Germany. Transactional booking emails are delivered through Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., United States), which receives the recipient address, relevant email content and delivery information. Cloudflare provides network delivery and security and processes connection and request information in its international network.

If we enable Google Calendar synchronization, appointment information is also sent to the Google calendar we select. An ordinary booking does not require you to sign in to Google. A page may load a business-selected image from its hosting provider; that provider receives the connection information needed to deliver the image. These service disclosures are for booking, communication, delivery and security, not for advertising audiences.

We may disclose the information necessary to comply with a binding legal requirement or lawful request from an authorized public authority. The Perelai operator describes its separate purposes for processing information in its own privacy notice: https://perelai.com/legal/privacy. That notice does not replace this business's notice.

We do not sell personal information collected through this booking process, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising. We disclose information to providers for the operational purposes described above. Submitting a booking does not authorize us to add your information to an advertising audience.

5. Online tracking and privacy signals

This booking service does not use advertising pixels or advertising trackers to follow you across unrelated websites. Operational providers may receive connection information when delivering or protecting the page, as described above; this does not mean that the page is free of browser storage or technical logs.

The booking application does not change its functional storage or necessary request processing in response to a browser Do Not Track signal. Global Privacy Control and other recognized opt-out preference signals are different from Do Not Track. Because this booking process does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context advertising, there is no such sale or sharing to switch off here, whether or not a signal is sent. This explanation does not waive any legally required response to an opt-out signal. Separately linked websites have their own practices.

6. Where information is processed and how long it is kept

The main hosting location is Germany. Email delivery through Resend involves processing in the United States. Cloudflare's network, optional Google Calendar synchronization and image delivery may involve other countries. Perelai's Ukrainian operator provides remote support and administration from different countries through protected connections. Information is therefore not stored or accessed only in the United States.

Perelai uses contractual processing terms, access restrictions and confidentiality and security measures with its providers. International processing does not remove the protections of applicable law. You can ask us or support@perelai.app about recipients and processing locations.

Requests that do not lead to a completed service are kept for no more than 90 calendar days after the request is closed, including rejection, cancellation or expiry of its confirmation or handling period. Unanswered requests are also subject to closure and this limit. If no visit took place, related contact information is deleted with the request unless it is needed for another current booking or a separately justified purpose.

Ordinary client records and service history are kept for no more than 24 calendar months after the last completed visit. Editing a client record alone does not restart this period. If there is a future agreed appointment, we retain only the information needed to arrange and provide it. Information no longer needed is removed; an earlier valid privacy request is considered separately.

After an authorized and confirmed instruction to delete our business workspace, Perelai deletes in-scope client personal information from active systems without undue delay and within 30 calendar days. Residual backup copies are kept out of ordinary use and deleted within the following 30 calendar days after active deletion, ordinarily no later than 60 calendar days after the confirmed instruction. Previously deleted information is excluded from ordinary use after a backup restore. Archiving a workspace is not the same as deleting it.

Separately, Perelai may keep minimal evidence of acceptance of its own terms, where necessary to establish, exercise or defend claims, for 1095 calendar days after active deletion of this workspace's client data. This is restricted evidence, not a copy of the client record or full service history. Evidence concerning only our business's rules or marketing permission is not retained by Perelai on that basis. A documented legal duty or specific ongoing dispute may require particular records to be retained for a different period, with access restricted and the need reviewed. Accounting records and technical security records follow their own applicable purposes and schedules; this is not a universal three-year tax rule.

7. Privacy requests and choices

You can contact {{PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL}} to ask about the personal information we hold about you or request a correction or deletion. We review requests in light of applicable law and the records we are permitted or required to retain. We may need information reasonably necessary to verify your identity before disclosing, changing, or deleting personal information. We will explain if we cannot fulfill a request in full.

Depending on where you live and which laws apply to this business, you may have additional rights, such as access to a copy of your information, correction, deletion, portability, or choices about particular uses or disclosures. The request procedure is set out below. Nothing in this notice limits rights you have under applicable law.

We comply with US state privacy laws where they apply to this business and the processing concerned, taking account of each law's scope, applicable revenue or data-volume thresholds, activities and exemptions. Some privacy, security and consumer-protection duties apply regardless of business size. Perelai's responsibilities as a provider processing information for a business are assessed separately from that business's own responsibilities.

Where applicable law provides them, your rights may include access, correction, deletion, a portable copy, and the ability to opt out of sale, targeted-advertising sharing or certain profiling, or to limit particular uses of sensitive information. The sale and advertising practices of this booking process are described above. We do not penalize you for exercising rights protected by law.

Send requests to {{PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL}}. An authorized agent may contact us for you where permitted; we may request reasonable proof of authority and any identity verification the law allows. We respond within applicable deadlines and explain any lawful refusal or extension. To ask us to reconsider a refusal or exercise an applicable appeal right, reply to the decision or email the same address with the subject Privacy appeal. You may also complain to the privacy regulator or attorney general responsible for your state. Mandatory additional request methods or disclosures, where required for a particular business, are not replaced by this email process.

8. Automated decisions

The service checks availability, booking lead time, required fields, email confirmation and anti-abuse limits. Depending on our settings and those of the selected service provider, a valid request may be accepted automatically or sent to us for review. These checks can prevent submission or affect whether and when an appointment is confirmed. We do not use this booking process for advertising profiling or decisions about credit, employment, insurance or medical eligibility. If a check prevents you from booking or you want a person to review an outcome, contact {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

9. Changes to this notice

We publish a new dated version on this booking privacy page when we update the notice for future bookings. For a material change affecting information already held, we notify affected clients using their available booking contact details before applying the change, and obtain permission where required by law. Previously confirmed versions are not silently replaced. An updated notice does not itself authorize a new use of previously collected information.

10. Applicable law and the platform relationship

The separate relationship with the Perelai operator is governed by Ukrainian law, subject to mandatory protections under the law that applies to you, including applicable US state law. Nothing in this notice waives those protections or restricts a right to contact a regulator. This notice does not select Ukrainian law for the services provided by our business or replace the separate terms governing those services.
```
