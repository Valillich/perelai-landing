# CA/en — small-business operator procedure

**Current code scope:** CA/en is ACTIVE_IN_CODE_NOT_DEPLOYED for MB, NB, NL, NS, NT, NU,
ON, PE, SK and YT; QC/AB/BC remain closed. AB/BC-specific instructions below are future-market
preparation. General privacy requests, pending-access preservation and incident duties still
apply to the admitted cohort. Code activation is not evidence that these operations were exercised.

**Prepared:** 2026-09-26. A procedure to adopt and exercise before CA operation, not evidence
that an inbox, preservation control or message workflow has already been tested. Use the shared
Perelai operator runbooks; no new scheduler, helpdesk product or paid external representative is
required merely to perform these tasks. Scope: ordinary private-sector booking outside Québec.

## Responsibility and requests

For Perelai, assign ILLICHOV VALERII to handle support@perelai.app and record who covers absence.
For a SOLO Business, the owner can be Privacy Officer; STUDIO names a responsible person.
The published privacy email may equal the booking contact. Owner confirmation must mean that
the inbox is monitored and requests are routed to the responsible person. The designation is
accountability, not permission to ignore requests made through another appropriate channel.
[OPC accountability](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/p_principle/principles/p_accountability/).

Maintain a restricted register: case ID, received date, responsible Business, verified contact,
request scope, applicable jurisdiction, deadline, affected records/preservation reference,
actions, response date and closure/review date. Do not copy whole CRM records into a spreadsheet.
Verify proportionately, locate relevant data across active systems/providers/exports, redact
other people's information and respond securely. Workspace export alone is not necessarily a
safe individual access response. Ordinary requests are free under the proposed service policy.

Use a **30-calendar-day operational target**. This is not the statutory deadline of every province:

| Applicable route | Normal access response deadline | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| PIPEDA | 30 calendar days | Substantive response; extensions only on statutory grounds with timely notice |
| Alberta PIPA | 45 days | Provincial extension/notice rules; apply the shorter operational target |
| BC PIPA | 30 days as defined by BC law, excluding Saturdays/holidays | Do not translate this to a new universal 30-calendar-day legal rule; operational target remains shorter |

Sources: [OPC access](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/accessing-personal-information/obligations-for-organizations/02_05_d_54_ati_02/),
[AB access](https://www.alberta.ca/accessing-your-personal-information),
[BC Act ss.1,29–31](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/03063_01).
Correction, complaints and withdrawal have their own applicable duties; do not delay a readily
made correction just because an access deadline is longer. Explain refusals and review routes.
Keep a contested-correction notation and notify recipients when required. Ask for clarification
promptly; do not silently restart the clock. Escalate a threatened missed deadline before expiry.

## Preservation and ordinary cleanup

Before running cleanup, individual erasure, Company deletion or restore replay, check the
restricted register for pending access/review, a documented dispute/duty, and applicable BC
decision records. PIPEDA access records must survive long enough to exercise the right.
BC decision information may require one year from use, not from later Company deletion.
Record only the necessary information, reason, date and release/review deadline. Restrict use;
release for deletion when the obligation ends. No blanket retention of all CRM rows is authorised.
The current app does not yet demonstrate this record-specific CA protection; code or a verified
operator-controlled preservation path must protect the records before BC admission. Merely
skipping every deletion forever would contradict both the notice and minimisation.

Ordinary 90-day requests, 24-month client history and 30/30 workspace deletion remain the shared
baseline. Existing operator CLI skips require documented resolution, not a claim of successful
erasure. Keep the 1095-day Perelai assent evidence schedule distinct from these local duties.

## Overseas providers: reuse facts, verify variable locations

Maintain one restricted current inventory and make the relevant written policies available
on request. For each provider: purpose/data, role, countries, contractual safeguards, source/date
checked, change owner. Known facts reused: Hetzner in Germany; Resend/Plus Five Five, Inc. in
the United States; Ukrainian registration of the FOP; Cloudflare global delivery; optional
Google Calendar and external image delivery. Registered Ukraine address does not establish
where today's admin login originates. Record real support/admin countries and provider locations
from evidence; do not fabricate them or claim SCCs settle every Canadian transfer question.

For AB, confirm the country/purpose inventory and the written notice of how to get these policies
and reach the responsible person before collection. The template supplies the Privacy Officer
route. The generic word “global” alone does not complete the country inventory. If AB-specific
facts are not ready, keep AB closed while a properly scoped first Canadian cohort proceeds.
[Alberta requirements](https://www.alberta.ca/organization-responsibilities-for-protecting-personal-information).

## Incident handling

Contain promptly, preserve minimal evidence, notify the affected Business promptly as processor,
identify who controls the affected information and assess harm. Under PIPEDA, report to OPC and
notify individuals as soon as feasible when the statutory real-risk-of-significant-harm test is
met; cooperate on mitigation. Do not replace that test with GDPR's 72-hour rule. A small incident
may still be reportable. AB has its own reporting/notification route; BC duties must be assessed
under its applicable law rather than assuming identical mandatory reporting rules.

For PIPEDA-covered information, keep a restricted record of **every safeguards breach for 24
months from determining that it occurred**, including the decision not to report and reasons.
This is an incident record, not permission to retain a full leaked database or all booking data.
Assign its review/deletion date separately from Company cleanup. Manual incident records are
acceptable if complete, protected and available when required.
[OPC breach guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-for-businesses/privacy-breaches-at-your-business/gd_pb_201810/),
[Breach Regulations s.6](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-64/FullText.html).

## Email and imported contacts

Before enabling a CA message, record its purpose/content, recipient trigger, consent or precise
exception, sender details, unsubscribe route and evidence. A direct requested response can have
a full section 6 exception; a message only facilitating an agreed transaction may be exempt
from consent but still need identification/unsubscribe. Assess the actual content, including
links and offers, not the provider's “transactional” label.

For messages needing unsubscribe, a simple monitored reply/address can be used if genuinely easy,
free and effective. Keep the mechanism available for at least 60 days after the message and act
without delay, at most 10 business days. Preserve minimal suppression information so imports,
retries or later jobs do not restart the messages; define its separate purpose/review schedule.
Do not promise a reply-based route if the actual mailbox is no-reply or unmonitored. Until this
works, disable the affected optional messages. No automatic marketing to imported clients.
[CRTC guidance](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm),
[CRTC exception examples](https://www.crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/info.htm).

When importing a client's existing contact/history, the Business must have authority for the
original collection and continued use and give any required information; uploading a CSV does
not create consent. Never turn the import into marketing permission or health-data authorisation.
Use the same restrictions for manual entry and Calendar sync. A change affecting existing data
requires appropriate notice/consent; the current app does not claim an automated notification job.
