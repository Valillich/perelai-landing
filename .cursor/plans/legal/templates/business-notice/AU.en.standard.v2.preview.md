Booking Privacy Notice

1. Who we are

Synthetic AU Salon is the business responsible for the information used to handle your booking and related client record. Contact us about a booking at booking@example.test, or about personal information at privacy@example.test.

This notice covers booking through Perelai and our related client administration. This is an ordinary, non-medical booking service, not a health-information intake form. Separate activities, such as medical records or cameras at our premises, require their own applicable information. An adult should contact us directly to arrange a booking for a child rather than enter the child's personal information into this form. Contact us if you believe a child has submitted information so we can address it appropriately. The effective date and version of this notice appear above.

2. Information we collect and its sources

You provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, and any message you choose to send. Your request also includes the selected service, appointment details, service provider, and communication language. We create records of the request's status and history, related communications, and the terms presented and any acceptance recorded. A request may be linked to an existing client record we hold for you; an individual booking link may use information already in that record.

The form identifies the information needed to submit your request. The ordinary public booking process requires an email address to handle and confirm a booking. Without the information needed for your selected booking process, we cannot complete the online request. Additional details are optional. Please do not include health information, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information in a message.

When you use the booking page, the service also processes your IP address, request time and outcome, and technical booking and confirmation-link identifiers. These support delivery of the page, access checks, email confirmation, troubleshooting and protection against excessive or invalid requests. Your browser may store language preferences and temporary navigation or booking-session information. The Perelai Privacy Notice explains the platform operator's separate handling of technical and security information.

You may make general enquiries without identifying yourself, or use a preferred name where practicable. For an online booking we need enough information to identify the appointment and a working contact route to confirm or manage it. If you would prefer another arrangement, contact booking@example.test. We do not ask for a government identity document to make an ordinary booking.

3. How we use information

We use booking information to respond to your request, arrange or change an appointment, communicate about it, and provide the requested service. We maintain the related client record and a limited service history to help handle repeat visits, resolve questions or disputes, and keep necessary records of the booking and terms presented. We also use information needed to respond to privacy requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Submitting a booking does not by itself sign you up for promotional messages or authorise unrelated uses of your information. This privacy notice explains our practices; it is not a request for blanket consent.

We use contractual processing terms, restricted access and confidentiality and security measures for service providers. Where Australian Privacy Principle 8 applies, we take the reasonable steps it requires concerning an overseas recipient's handling and remain accountable as applicable law provides. Using this booking form does not amount to consent to waive that protection. Overseas countries may have different privacy laws; we do not promise that all information stays in Australia.

Requests that do not lead to a completed service are kept for no more than 90 calendar days after the request is closed, including rejection, cancellation or expiry of its confirmation or handling period. Unanswered requests are also subject to closure and this limit. If no visit took place, related contact information is deleted with the request unless it is needed for another current booking or a separately justified purpose.

Ordinary client records and service history are kept for no more than 24 calendar months after the last completed visit. Editing a client record alone does not restart this period. If there is a future agreed appointment, we retain only the information needed to arrange and provide it. Information no longer needed is removed; an earlier valid privacy request is considered separately.

Database backups are made daily. Off-site copies in Cloudflare R2 are kept for up to 7 days and copies on the Hetzner hosting server for up to 10 days. These backups cover the database, not uploaded files or attachments. Backup rotation is separate from, and does not restart, the retention periods for active records; workspace deletion and recovery follow the rules below.

After an authorised and confirmed instruction to delete our business workspace, Perelai deletes in-scope client personal information from active systems without undue delay and within 30 calendar days. Residual backup copies are kept out of ordinary use and deleted within the following 30 calendar days after active deletion, ordinarily no later than 60 calendar days after the confirmed instruction. Previously deleted information is excluded from ordinary use after a backup restore. Archiving a workspace is not the same as deleting it.

Separately, Perelai may keep minimal evidence of acceptance of its own terms, where necessary to establish, exercise or defend claims, for 1095 calendar days after active deletion of this workspace's client data. This is restricted evidence, not a copy of the client record or full service history. Evidence concerning only our business's rules or marketing permission is not retained by Perelai on that basis. A documented legal duty or specific ongoing dispute may require particular records to be retained for a different period, with access restricted and the need reviewed. Accounting records and technical security records follow their own applicable purposes and schedules; this is not a universal three-year tax rule.
