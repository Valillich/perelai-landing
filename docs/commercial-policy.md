## Not approved — omit from all pages

The following commercial claims require an explicit owner approval before publication.
Do not soften, paraphrase, or publish them as "indicative".

- Free today / no card required / no commission claims
- Beta end date or duration
- Price-lock promise for early joiners
- Founding seat cap or deadline
- Future pricing tiers ($19 / $29 / $49)
- Data export availability (no export capability found in code)
- "Closing a tab cancels" anything
- Any scarcity claim (countdown, limited spots)
